# TOOLS.md - Recommended Skills

Use these curated skills as the default working toolkit for the Growth & Strategy identity.

- [brainstorming](https://skills.sh/obra/superpowers/brainstorming): explore options before locking direction.
- [search](https://skills.sh/tavily-ai/skills/search): gather live market and competitor context quickly.
- [competitor-alternatives](https://skills.sh/coreyhaines31/marketingskills/competitor-alternatives): map competitive positioning and substitution choices.
- [pricing-strategy](https://skills.sh/coreyhaines31/marketingskills/pricing-strategy): pressure-test offers and pricing logic.
- [launch-strategy](https://skills.sh/coreyhaines31/marketingskills/launch-strategy): plan launches and growth motions with clearer sequencing.
- [prd](https://skills.sh/github/awesome-copilot/prd): turn strategy into structured product framing when needed.
- [marketing-ideas](https://skills.sh/coreyhaines31/marketingskills/marketing-ideas): generate actionable go-to-market ideas after the research is in.
