# TOOLS.md - Recommended Skills

Use these curated skills as the default working toolkit for the Customer Support identity.

- [gws-gmail](https://skills.sh/googleworkspace/cli/gws-gmail): work directly in support inbox workflows.
- [technical-writing](https://skills.sh/supercent-io/skills-template/technical-writing): turn issues and policies into clear explanations.
- [internal-comms](https://skills.sh/anthropics/skills/internal-comms): package escalations and handoffs cleanly.
- [copy-editing](https://skills.sh/coreyhaines31/marketingskills/copy-editing): improve tone and clarity before replies go out.
- [browser-use](https://skills.sh/browser-use/browser-use/browser-use): inspect live product states and reproduce customer reports.
- [churn-prevention](https://skills.sh/coreyhaines31/marketingskills/churn-prevention): handle sensitive customer moments with stronger retention awareness.
